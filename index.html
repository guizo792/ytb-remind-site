How it works

  1. 1

    Click the bell, or the toolbar icon

    On YouTube the bell sits beside Like and Share, and in the three-dot menu of every video card. On any other page, click the toolbar icon, press Alt+Shift+R, or right-click and choose Remind me later.

  2. 2

    Pick a time

    20 minutes, tomorrow, next week, or any date. Add a note so you remember why.

  3. 3

    Get nudged

    A notification arrives, and one click takes you back: the video at the same second, the article at the sentence where you stopped.
