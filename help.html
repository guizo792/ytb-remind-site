Help and support

Remind Me Later lets you set a timed reminder, with a note, on any YouTube video or Short, and notifies you when it's due.

Something not working?

Tell me what happened. Open the extension's page in the Chrome Web Store, go to its Reviews tab and say which browser you use, what you did, and what you expected to happen. I read them all.

Open the Reviews page

Reviews are public. Please don't include personal information. You sign in with your Google account, the one you already use in Chrome.

Common questions

I didn't get a notification

Reminders fire while your browser is running; if it was closed, you'll be notified when it starts. Also check that your system isn't in Do Not Disturb or Focus mode and that notifications are allowed for your browser in your system settings. Even when a notification is hidden, the number on the extension's toolbar icon shows how many reminders are due, and they're all listed in its popup.

Where are my reminders stored?

On your own device, in your browser's extension storage. They aren't sent anywhere and they don't sync to other computers. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

Cancel or dismiss individual reminders in the extension's popup. To remove everything, uninstall the extension, which deletes all of its stored data.

Which browsers does it work in?

Chrome and other Chromium-based desktop browsers, such as Brave. It doesn't work in phone browsers or in the YouTube app.

A button or menu entry is missing on YouTube

Reload the YouTube tab after installing or updating the extension. If it's still missing, YouTube may have changed its page; please report it above and mention which page you were on.
